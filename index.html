How to Play

Goal: capture the enemy HQ 🏰 or destroy every enemy unit.

Move: tap a unit, tap a blue tile, then pick an action. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Attack: choose Fire, tap a target, check the damage forecast, confirm. Artillery and Rockets can't move and fire in the same turn — and can't be countered.

Capture: Infantry and Mechs capture cities, bases and HQs. A full-health unit needs 2 turns. Each property pays 1,000G every day.

Build: tap one of your Bases or Airports to buy units with your funds.

Terrain: Woods and Mountains raise defense (★). Roads speed up wheels. Only air units cross the sea; Mechs climb mountains easily.

Heal: end a turn on a friendly property to repair 2 HP (costs funds). Damaged twins can Join into one unit.

Transports: APCs and T-Copters carry foot soldiers. Load by moving onto them, Drop to unload.

Scout: tap an enemy unit to see its threat range.

Counters: Tanks beat vehicles · Mechs beat tanks cheaply · Anti-Air shreds copters & infantry · Bombers erase ground · Fighters own the sky · Artillery softens everything.
